# Benjamin tenOever

Jan T. Vilcek Professor of Molecular Pathogenesis, Chair of the Department of Microbiology, and Director of the NYU Langone Virology Institute at NYU Grossman School of Medicine and NYU Langone Health.

I study how cells sense and respond to virus infection, and how those virus-host interactions can be turned into tools. My laboratory works across innate antiviral immunity and the interferon response, RNA biology and RNA-based antiviral defense including RNA interference, influenza virus and other respiratory RNA viruses, viral immune evasion, and the engineering of programmable viral vectors for cell-specific RNA delivery.

### Research themes

- Virus-host interactions and innate antiviral immunity (interferon signaling)
- Influenza virus genome regulation and small viral RNAs
- RNA-mediated antiviral defense and the reconstruction of antiviral RNAi
- Viral immune recognition and evasion
- Programmable virology: engineered Sendai and other vectors for RNA delivery
- Respiratory virus biology and pandemic host response
